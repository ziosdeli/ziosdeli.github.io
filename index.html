    Our Story

    Your neighborhood "zio"

    In Italian, zio means uncle - and that's exactly the welcome you'll get. We're a family-run Italian deli and neighborhood Italian eatery in the heart of Croton-on-Hudson, Westchester County, serving fresh hero sandwiches, store-made fresh mozzarella, imported specialties and honest, made-from-scratch food to our neighbors along the Hudson.

    Grabbing coffee before the Metro-North, feeding the office, or catering the block party - pull up a stool. You're family here.

    "Mangia bene, vivi bene."

    Eat well, live well.

    What sets us apart is simple: quality ingredients, generous portions, and service from a place that knows your name. Store-made fresh mozzarella, store-made chicken cutlets and salads, imported prosciutto and soppressata you won't find at the supermarket.

    Stop in - better yet, become a regular.

    Good to Know

    Frequently asked questions

    Where is Zio's Italian Deli located?
    We're at 2040 Albany Post Road in Croton-on-Hudson, NY 10520 - on Route 9A with convenient parking, minutes from the Croton-Harmon Metro-North station.
    What are your hours?
    Monday-Friday 6:00 AM-6:00 PM, Saturday 7:00 AM-4:00 PM, and Sunday 8:00 AM-2:00 PM. We open early for coffee and breakfast on the go.
    Do you offer catering in Westchester?
    Yes - we cater office lunches, parties and holidays with pasta and hot trays, 3- and 6-foot party heroes, antipasto and cold-cut platters throughout Croton-on-Hudson and nearby Westchester. Please give at least 3 days' notice. Call (914) 930-8535 to plan your order.
    Can I call ahead to order a sandwich?
    Absolutely - call (914) 930-8535 and we'll have your order ready for pickup. It's the fastest way to skip the lunch rush.
    What's your most popular hero?
    The Zio's Combo (prosciutto, capocolla, soppressata, pepperoni and fresh mozzarella) and the Chicken Cutlet are neighborhood favorites. Don't miss our store-made fresh mozzarella and daily store-made soups.
    Is Zio's an Italian restaurant or a deli?
    Both - Zio's is a family-run Italian deli and casual Italian eatery in Croton-on-Hudson serving made-to-order Italian sandwiches, hot food, store-made salads and store-made fresh mozzarella, plus full catering. Order at the counter for takeout or pickup.
    Do you make your own fresh mozzarella?
    Yes - we make fresh mozzarella in-store daily, along with store-made chicken cutlets, salads and soups. It's the heart of our Italian deli and a Croton-on-Hudson favorite.
