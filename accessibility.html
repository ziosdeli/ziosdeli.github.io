Accessibility Statement

Zio's Italian Deli is committed to making ziosdeli.com welcoming and usable for everyone, including people with disabilities.

Our standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA - the widely recognized standard for making websites accessible to people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, and other assistive technologies.

What we have done

  • Our full menu is written as real text on the page, so screen readers can read every item and price.
  • The whole site works with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator and a "skip to main content" link.
  • Images have descriptive text alternatives, and decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text and controls meet AA color-contrast levels, and we respect the "reduced motion" setting.
  • Clear headings and structure make it easy to move around the page.

Ongoing effort

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We review the site periodically and work to fix any issues we find.

Need help, or found a problem?

If you ever have trouble using this website or placing an order, please contact us directly - we are always glad to help, including taking your order by phone or email.
